Freeze

Brave · Chrome · Edge

Pause
what's playing.

One toolbar click pauses playing video and audio across every tab — especially YouTube. Click again and only those resume.

No accounts · No network · No tracking · Open tabs only

  1. 01
    Download freeze.zip

    Unzip it anywhere — Desktop is fine.

  2. 02

    Enable Developer mode (top-right toggle), then click Load unpacked.

  3. 03

    Select the unzipped folder.

    Pin Freeze to the toolbar. Done.
